How it works

About a day, start to finish

  1. 1

    We meet

    Optional · 30 minutes

    A short call to understand the decision, what’s riding on it and what you’ve already tried. Prefer to skip it? You can start straight from the form.

  2. 2

    We think it through, async

    24 hours · voice notes or text

    Voice notes or text, whenever you have a moment. I ask the questions that matter and use decision tools to separate what you actually know from what you’re guessing, check which biases might be steering you, and name what would change your mind. Everything can be fully anonymised, so you never have to share confidential details.

  3. 3

    You get your decision brief

    One page

    One page with what you know, what you’re guessing, the biases we checked, what would change your mind, and the call. Ready to act on, or to forward to leadership as your reasoning.

Hannah had a decision to make the same day: whether, and how, to keep working with a client.

Who it’s for

Is this the right fit?
