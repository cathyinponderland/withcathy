What's included
1

A pre-mortem, before anything gets built

We imagine the launch has already happened and work backwards from the numbers.

We look at your list and audience, your conversion rates from past launches of the same or similar offers (or industry benchmarks if it's your first), and every step of the journey: how many people come to the free or paid event, how many buy the upsell, how many move on to the main offer. Then we subtract the costs.

We map three scenarios, best case, worst case and most likely, and use them to decide on price points, whether your first event should be free or paid, and whether you need more launch events to reach your goal. We also write down every assumption we're making.

Running the numbers can feel like it might kill the launch. It rarely does. Usually it changes the price, the format or the number of events, and it's much better to find that out now than on day three of cart open.

2

The launch plan

A timeline with every asset, every date and every handoff, so you always know what's due, who's doing it and what's next.

3

The full tech build

Event and sales pages, checkout, upsells, email automations, course or membership setup and integrations. All built, tested and checked before anything goes live.

4

Copy editing

You or your copywriter write the copy. I edit it for clarity and structure, and make sure the message stays consistent from the first email to the checkout page.

5

Tracking while the launch is live

Analytics set up across the whole funnel. During the launch, we compare what's happening with the pre-mortem, so you know early whether you're on track and which lever to pull if you're not.

A post-mortem, after the doors close

We put the assumptions from the pre-mortem next to the real data and compare the launch with your previous ones. You come away knowing what worked, what didn't and which variables to test next time, so every launch makes the next one easier.

FAQ

Why hourly and not a package?

Launches vary a lot. One free workshop and one sales page is a very different job from a summit, a challenge and three upsells. Hourly means you pay for your launch, not an average one, and the estimate after the pre-mortem means you're never guessing.

Do you write the copy?

No. You or your copywriter write it, and I edit it: tightening, restructuring and keeping everything consistent.

Do you create the launch strategy from scratch?

Not in this offer. The pre-mortem helps you decide on prices, event formats and how many launch events you need, based on your numbers. The bigger positioning and offer strategy stays with you or your strategist.

What if the pre-mortem says the launch won't work?

Then we've found out before spending the money and the time. Usually it means changing the price, the format or the number of events, not cancelling.

I'm a coach. Can we work together?

If your methods are grounded in evidence and you have relevant training, yes. If they're based on astrology, human design, numerology or similar, I'm not the right fit.

What if I use a tool you haven't listed?

Tell me on the fit call. I pick up new tools quickly.
